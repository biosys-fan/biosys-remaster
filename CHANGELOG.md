# Changelog

## 0.1.0: first public release

The first build anyone can simply download and play.

**Download:** `Biosys-Remaster-0.1.0-macOS.dmg` (Intel and Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later).

### How to start

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Biosys Remaster** onto **Applications**. Open it from there.
2. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app, because this free fan project has no paid Apple developer
   signature. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway**. Once only.
3. The app asks for the original game, which is not included. Its start screen opens the download page
   (<https://www.myabandonware.com/game/biosys-f2f#downloads>): download **Game CD** and **Setup CD** and drop both
   files onto the app's window. No unpacking. The app copies what it needs (about 1 GB) and starts the game.

In the game, **F10** (or **Cmd+,**) opens the settings with the remaster options.

### In this release

- The complete game: engine, rules, the BioSys 4 terminal, films, sound, saving and loading in the original format.
- First-run screen that takes the two original CD images by drag and drop (zip, disc image, or unpacked folder).
- Optional remaster features, all off by default: smooth and widescreen panoramas, clean pictures, sharp text,
  a full-height interface, an enhanced sound path with volume groups, autosave, 40 save slots, an in-game
  settings menu, adjustable pace and mouse sensitivity.
- Optional simulation levels (Faithful, Corrected, Closed loop) and difficulty levels (Guided, Original, Survivor),
  chosen on the New game screen and stored in each save.

### Known limits

- macOS only. Windows is planned (see TODO.md).
- The app is not notarised by Apple, hence the "Open Anyway" step.
- Much of the remaster layer has been verified by scripts and measurements, not by long play. Section 10.3 of the
  manual lists what has and has not been seen on a real screen.

This is an unofficial homage to *Biosys* (1999, JumpStart Solutions / Take-Two Interactive), not affiliated with or
endorsed by the original creators or rights holders. It contains no file of the original game. See DISCLAIMER.md.
